# Terms of Use / HRMS Acceptable Use (Outline) — G-Architects HRMS

**Status:** Draft template — NOT for publication until HR policy owner + counsel validate  
**Version:** v0.1 (draft)

---

## 1. Authorized use
The HRMS is for authorized employees, HR, managers, team leads and administrators of G Architects. Access is role-based; do not share credentials.

## 2. Account security
- Keep your password confidential; use the password-change flow if compromised
- Passwords are hashed; reset OTPs are single-use and expire in 5 minutes
- Sessions are revoked on password change (token_version)

## 3. Accurate data entry
- Enter accurate, complete information in your profile, attendance, leave and work-log records
- Do not submit false records or impersonate another employee

## 4. Prohibited misuse
- No unauthorized access to another employee's data (changing IDs in the URL is access control violation)
- No scraping, automation, or attempts to bypass authentication/authorization
- No uploading of unlawful, malicious, or third-party-infringing content in documents/photos

## 5. Acceptable uploads
- Documents and attendance photos only, within size/type limits
- Do not upload another person's personal data without authority

## 6. Intellectual property
- Company records and documents remain G Architects property
- Employee retains rights to personal content as per employment contract

## 7. Availability & support
- The HRMS is provided on an "as available" basis; maintenance windows may apply
- Support via HR / admin contact; no SLA guaranteed unless agreed in writing

## 8. Suspension & termination
- Access is revoked when employment status changes to inactive/paused/terminated
- HR/Admin may suspend accounts for policy violation; last-active-admin is protected from deletion

## 9. Disclaimers
- Not legal advice; does not override the employment contract or statutory rights
- No warranty of uninterrupted service; liability limited as per applicable law
