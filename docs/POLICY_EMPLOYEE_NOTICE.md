# Employee Privacy Notice (Outline) — G-Architects HRMS

**Status:** Draft template — NOT for publication until Indian counsel validates  
**Owner:** HR policy owner + privacy/legal reviewer  
**Version:** v0.1 (draft)  
**Audience:** Employees of G Architects

---

## 1. Purpose of this notice
Plain-language HR-specific notice explaining what employee data the HRMS collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and your rights. Complements the public Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Attendance photos & location
- At check-in, you may be asked for camera (selfie) and location permission. These are requested **only when you invoke check-in**, never in the background.
- We explain: why they are needed, whether coordinates are stored or only a permission/value is checked, who can view them (you, your manager/HR, admin), retention, and what to do if permission fails.
- If camera/location is required for your attendance method and you cannot provide it, a documented exception / manual-review workflow is available — you will not be silently denied all attendance recording.

---

## 3. Your employee record
- Identity, contact, emergency contact, DOB, gender, address, qualifications, languages, marital status
- Employment: department, designation, reporting manager, joining/last-working date, role, status
- You can view and request correction of your own record via My Profile. Some fields (role, status, reporting line) are changed by HR/Admin only.

---

## 4. Payroll, bank & statutory data
- Salary components, bank account/IFSC, PAN, Aadhaar/passport, UAN/PF/ESI
- Collected only when there is a documented payroll/statutory purpose
- Access restricted sharply: HR/Admin; you can view your own payslip and compensation summary
- Never included in email subjects, push text, or error logs

---

## 5. Manager / HR access
- Managers/team leads see only their reporting tree and assigned projects/units — not every employee's data
- HR handles people modules (leave, documents, onboarding, attendance review) within approved scope
- Admin has defined administrative scope; money-write and structural changes are admin-only

---

## 6. Work logs & monitoring boundaries
- Daily work updates and project status updates are work records you or your lead create
- Managers can see updates from their reporting tree; this is for work coordination, not surveillance
- No keylogging, screen capture, or off-hours tracking

---

## 7. Retention
- Attendance photos: short-term, auto-purged
- Reset OTPs: 5 minutes, single-use
- Audit logs: retained per legal requirement (append-only)
- Payroll/statutory: per accounting/tax law
- Full schedule: docs/POLICY_RETENTION_DELETION.md

---

## 8. Your rights & escalation
- View/correct your data; withdraw optional consent; raise a grievance
- Contact: HR contact (to be inserted by counsel); escalation timeline to be inserted

---

## 9. Consent vs employment processing
- Some processing is necessary for your employment (payroll, attendance, statutory filings) and does not depend on a checkbox
- Optional processing (e.g. push notifications) uses separate consent records; you can decline/withdraw
- Refusing optional consent does not block unrelated HRMS functions

*This outline is an engineering draft, not legal advice. Indian counsel must validate before publication.*
